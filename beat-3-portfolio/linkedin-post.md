For my CodePath project, I wrote rules for a tool that reviews a draft before I submit a code change. I used it to check my draft for a fix that helps hide phone numbers in text.

The first review showed me that I had left out two check results. I ran both checks and updated my draft: the formatting check passed, and one website test timed out twice. After I reported both results, the tool accepted the updated draft and I opened the pull request.

This taught me that a good handoff includes what worked and what still needs attention. That way, the next person can make a decision based on what actually happened.
